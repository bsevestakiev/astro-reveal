Starting today, Reveal users can connect directly to their Databricks environment and build real-time dashboards using our fully embedded analytics experience.

## Native Databricks Integration

Databricks users can now embed real-time insights directly into their applications without extra steps or added complexity.

As a cloud-based data platform built on **Apache Spark**and **ML tools**, Databricks powers large-scale analytics. This new connector makes it easy to turn that data into live, in-app dashboards—fully integrated and ready for users.

**Teams can now:**

- Databricks data in just a few lines of code
- Build and share dashboards with custom visualizations
- Embed analytics directly into your app with Reveal’s SDK
- Use Reveal AI to ask natural-language questions of your Databricks data

With Reveal, those insights are no longer siloed—they’re delivered natively inside the applications teams are building.
