## Why Evaluate Sisense for Embedded Analytics?

Embedded analytics has become a critical requirement for modern SaaS products. Whether you’re building an application from the ground up or adding dashboards into an existing application, the ability to seamlessly integrate analytics into your UX—without compromising speed, scale, or flexibility—can significantly impact user experience and development velocity.

Sisense is one of the more recognizable names in this space. Known for its enterprise reach and powerful backend capabilities, it’s often shortlisted by product leaders and technical teams exploring embedded BI platforms.

But how well does it serve teams focused on customer-facing embedding, developer control, and fast time-to-value?

![Sisense Embedded Analytics Alternative](/images/2022/07/sisense-embedded-alternative.png)

## Pros: What Sisense Embedded Analytics Does Well

Sisense brings several strengths to the table, especially for enterprise organizations focused on internal BI and complex data modeling:
**Here are the core strengths that make Sisense a popular choice:**

**Flexible Data Modeling:** Sisense’s ElastiCube engine enables teams to combine and prepare data from various sources into high-performing, reusable models.
**Extensibility via Plugins & APIs:** With a variety of plugin frameworks and APIs, developers can customize dashboards and behaviors to a moderate extent.
**White-Labeling & Branding Options:** Sisense supports theming and styling to align dashboards with your brand—useful for semi-custom embedded experiences.
**Enterprise-Grade Governance:** Built-in security, role-based access, and SSO integration make Sisense a good fit for organizations with strict data control needs.
**Deployment Flexibility:** Options for on-premises, hybrid, or cloud deployments give enterprises control over data residency and compliance requirements.

These strengths make Sisense appealing for teams looking to roll out dashboards across internal departments or where data security and modeling are paramount.

## Cons: Key Limitations or Concerns Users Have Raised

While Sisense offers powerful backend capabilities, it often introduces friction when used for embedding analytics into external, customer-facing products. Common challenges include:

**Here are the most common challenges teams report when working with Sisense:**

- **Complex Setup and Learning Curve:** Initial setup—including modeling, configuration, and deployment—often requires significant time and technical expertise.
- **Performance at Scale:** Slower load times and performance bottlenecks as dashboard complexity or usage increases.
- **Limited Front-End Customization:** Most embedding is iFrame-based, making it difficult to fully control the UX or integrate dashboards deeply into your product’s UI.
- **High and Unpredictable Pricing:** Licensing structures can vary significantly depending on usage and user volume—making long-term cost planning difficult.
- **Inconsistent Developer Experience:** Inconsistent documentation and fragmented tooling as barriers to efficient development and customization.

These limitations are especially important for teams building data-driven apps that demand full customization, efficient workflows, and scalable pricing.
