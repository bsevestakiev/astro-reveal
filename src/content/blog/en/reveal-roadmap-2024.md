2024 is just around the corner, and we’re thrilled to share a sneak peek into what Reveal has in store for you! Our commitment to providing cutting-edge embedded analytics solutions remains stronger than ever. Get ready to be excited as we introduce many new features that will take your analytics game to the next level next year.

## 1. New Toolbar for Enhanced Visualization Control

The centerpiece of our 2024 feature lineup is the brand-new toolbar. Designed to empower you with greater control over your visualizations, the toolbar will be your go-to tool for optimizing data insights. Here’s a glimpse of what you can expect:

- **Constant Lines**: Easily add constant lines to your charts for improved data interpretation.
- **Trendlines:** Unlock the power of trend analysis with trendline capabilities.
- **Insights on Selection:** Get instant insights based on selected series, making data-driven decisions a breeze.
- **Outlier Detection:**Identify outliers effortlessly, enhancing data quality and accuracy.

…and more. The new toolbar will come packed with features to streamline your analytics experience to new heights.

## 2. Chart Percent-Based Filtering

We understand the importance of flexibility in data analysis. With our new chart percent-based filtering, you will be able to gain even more control over your data. Customize your filters based on percentages, enabling you to focus on the insights that matter most to you.

## 3. Data Paging

Efficient data handling is crucial for seamless analytics. Say goodbye to sluggish performance and embrace lightning-fast data access with our upcoming data paging feature which will ensure smooth navigation through large datasets.

## 4. Series Color Customization

Another exciting feature to expect in 2024 is series color customization. With it, you will be able to personalize your visualizations to match your brand or preferences and define specific colors for your series to create visually appealing and informative charts.

## 5. Category Level Colors Across the Dashboard

Consistency is key when presenting data. With category-level colors across the dashboard, you can ensure that specific values maintain the same color throughout, enhancing clarity and understanding.

## A Year in Review: 2023

In the past year, Reveal has been on a journey of continuous improvement, culminating in the [release of version 1.6.1](/blog/mongodb-reveal-release) of our [embedded analytics SDK](/download-sdk). This update brought MongoDB support, headless export capabilities, and enhanced security features, reinforcing our commitment to delivering seamless integration of analytics into your applications. In addition, our [1.6.0 release](/blog/reveal-1-6-0-release) introduced game-changing features like [DIY custom visualizations](/blog/diy-custom-data-visualizations), allowing users to tailor their dashboards with just a few lines of code. Our second game-changing feature of the 1.6.0 release, the interactive chart filtering, further revolutionizes data exploration, providing a dynamic and responsive experience that allows users to filter the entire dashboard on the fly.
