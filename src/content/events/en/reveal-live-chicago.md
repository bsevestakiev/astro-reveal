## Why This Event

**The shift isn't dashboards vs. AI — it's insight vs. action**

Most teams don't struggle to see data. They struggle to turn it into action inside their product.

What's changed in the last 12–18 months:

<ul class="list-checkmark fill-icon mb-4">
	<li>AI expectations have moved from chat → <strong>workflow + execution</strong></li>
	<li>Embedded analytics is now a core product capability, not a feature</li>
	<li>UX expectations demand <strong>speed, consistency, and trust</strong></li>
</ul>

At the same time:

<ul class="list-checkmark fill-icon mb-4">
	<li>Teams are rebuilding analytics and UI layers from scratch</li>
	<li>Decision workflows remain disconnected from actual product experiences</li>
<li>Governance and data trust are becoming blockers—not enablers</li>
</ul>

**This event is designed to fix that—practically, not theoretically.**
