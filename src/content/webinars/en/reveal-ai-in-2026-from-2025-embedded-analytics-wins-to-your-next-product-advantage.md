In 2025, development teams moved embedded analytics from pilots to production. In this live session, we will walk through the lessons learned from these real-world deployments and how they directly shaped the Q1 2026 roadmap for Reveal, Reveal AI, Ignite UI, and App Builder.

Join us to explore common use cases, friction points, and the latest governance and performance features. The session includes a live, open-source App Builder demo, providing a reusable starting point for building modern, analytics-ready user interfaces.

Whether you are a developer, Product Manager, or CTO, you will leave with a practical plan and a clear view of what is shipping next.
