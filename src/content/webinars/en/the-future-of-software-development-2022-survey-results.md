With 2021 firmly in hindsight, software developers and organizations faced another year of unprecedented disruptions, as they continued to address challenge after challenge in navigating a remote-first environment and a digital transformation of the workplace.

As we look to the future of software development in 2022, Reveal surveyed thousands of software developers and IT professionals across leading organizations to determine what their biggest challenges were, pain points affecting businesses, and areas for growth.

Join Reveal’s Product Manager, Casey McGuigan, and Senior Vice President of Developer Tools at Infragistics, Jason Beres, for a live webinar as they present our findings and share information for overcoming these obstacles in 2022 and beyond.
