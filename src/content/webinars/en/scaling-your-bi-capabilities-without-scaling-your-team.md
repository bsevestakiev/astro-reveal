Hiring BI talent is tough — and expensive. Yet the pressure to deliver data-rich product experiences keeps growing. And while the temptation to build analytics in-house is real, doing so often means pulling your most valuable engineers away from core product work — delaying innovation and inflating costs.

In this 30-minute webinar, we’ll show how modern SaaS companies are embedding self-service dashboards directly into their applications to meet demand faster and more efficiently — while keeping their teams focused on what matters most.

This webinar is tailored for CTOs and technical leaders looking to maximize impact without increasing BI overhead.
