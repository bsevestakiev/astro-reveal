### The Real Embedded Analytics Challenge Isn't Data

Most embedded analytics projects don't fail because of missing features. They fail because end users don't adopt them.

Product teams are stuck between:

<ul class="list-checkmark fill-icon ml-3">
<li>Giving users too much power and overwhelming them</li>
<li>Locking things down too tightly and limiting value</li>
</ul>

The result? Feature-rich analytics that feel complex, unused, or disconnected from the product experience.

### What This Webinar Is About

**Ease of Use as a Product Decision**

In this session, we'll show how leading ISVs design embedded analytics that feel intuitive for end users while staying fully controlled by product teams.

You'll learn how to:

<ul class="list-checkmark fill-icon ml-3">
<li>Decide what users can explore, edit, or customize — and when</li>
<li>Design analytics experiences for different user personas</li>
<li>Enable powerful interactions without custom development</li>
<li>Increase adoption, reduce support overhead, and speed time-to-value</li>
</ul>

This is a practical, product-focused discussion for teams responsible for analytics adoption and product experience.
