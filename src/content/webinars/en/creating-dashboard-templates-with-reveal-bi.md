Embedded analytics tools like Reveal help users create beautiful and effective dashboards with the right purpose, chart type, essential data, and effective interactions.

And furthermore, create dashboard templates to cut dashboard creation time in half.

Dashboard templates allow users to create new dashboards with similar KPIs, integrations and layouts, but different data sources – easily and quickly.

Join us for a templates-focused webinar and learn how to streamline the process of creating new dashboards that provide key business insights and drive users’ decision-making.

By the end of this webinar, you’ll have a better understanding of how to create dashboard templates that meet your organization's and stakeholders' needs and save time from building similar dashboards from scratch every time.
