        <br/><br/>
        Respondents identified security (<strong>51%</strong>), AI code reliability (<strong>45%</strong>), and data privacy (<strong>41%</strong>) among their biggest software development challenges for 2025.

      The Top Priority for 2025 is to Expand the Use of AI Within Organizations
    </h2>
<img alt="The Top Priority for 2025 is to Expand the Use of AI Within Organizations" class="b-lazy img-fluid" decoding="async" id="expand-ai-img" src="https://static.infragistics.com/marketing/reveal/whitepapers/2025/top-software-development-challenges/reveal-top-software-developlment-challenges-2025-charts_companys-expansion-plan.png" title="The Top Priority for 2025 is to Expand the Use of AI Within Organizations" width="100%"/>

<img alt="Insights" class="smush-detected-img smush-image-2" decoding="async" id="insights-icon-2" src="https://static.infragistics.com/marketing/reveal/whitepapers/2025/insights-icon.svg" title="Insights"/>
</div>
<div class="col-md-9 pr-0 pr-lg-5 mt-3">
<p id="insights-text-2" style="font-size:1.15rem">
        The main priority in 2025 is AI adoption, with <strong>73%</strong> of tech leaders planning to expand the use of AI within organizations in the next year. However, <strong>55%</strong> also find that AI deployment will be the biggest challenge they face.
        <br/><br/>
        Many companies initially adopted AI for automation and efficiency gains. Now, the focus shifts to <strong>stabilizing AI workflows, improving reliability, and securing AI applications.</strong> Those that fail to refine their implementation risk falling behind competitors who prioritize optimization.
      </p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="col-md-9 pr-0 pr-lg-5 mt-3">
<p id="insights-text-2" style="font-size:1.15rem">
        Tech leaders plan to optimize resource utilization in 2025 by integrating and expanding the use of AI (<strong>42%</strong>). Companies are not looking to replace workers with AI, rather incorporate these tools into their current workflows to help optimize task management.
        <br/><br/>
        Expanding into new markets, taking on new projects, and managing increased requests for proposals are among the <strong>lowest priorities</strong> for 2025, with <strong>less than 20%</strong> of tech leaders including them in their 2025 expansion plans.

    </h2>
<img alt="The Tech Talent Crisis Deepens" class="b-lazy img-fluid" decoding="async" id="tech-talent-img" src="https://static.infragistics.com/marketing/reveal/whitepapers/2025/top-software-development-challenges/reveal-top-software-developlment-challenges-2025-charts_tech-jobs-challenging-to-fill.png" title="The Tech Talent Crisis Deepens" width="100%"/>

</div>
<div class="col-md-9 pr-0 pr-lg-5 mt-3">
<p id="insights-text-2" style="font-size:1.15rem">
        The AI talent shortage, which saw some improvement in 2024, has worsened in 2025—especially in <strong>AI and cybersecurity roles</strong>. Companies that rapidly adopted AI now <strong>lack the specialized workforce</strong> needed to scale, refine, and secure their AI-driven infrastructure.
        <br/><br/>
        There is a strong demand for skilled AI engineers, with <strong>28% of tech leaders</strong> finding it challenging to fill AI engineer positions. While the adoption of AI has helped companies optimize workflows, it has also opened new job opportunities that they are still struggling to fill.
      </p>
</div>
</div>

        <br/><br/>
        The majority of tech leaders now believe that security can no longer be an afterthought—it must be <strong>integrated into AI development from the start</strong>. Companies need <strong>real-time threat detection, AI auditing, and compliance-driven security measures</strong> to stay ahead of evolving threats.
