<h2 class="wp-block-heading" class="wp-block-heading" id="meet-scriptly"><strong>Meet Scriptly</strong> </h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">When Brian Prigge became Chief Technology Officer for a large, national group of independent pharmacies, he realized they were working with antiquated software systems. He knew there had to be a more efficient pharmacy management technology solution. In 2020, he founded <a href="https://www.scriptly.tech/" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">Scriptly</a> to provide a modern, user-friendly solution to easily track prescriber trends, identify adherence issues, and analyze inventory forecasting. Scriptly has revolutionized operations at pharmacies of all sizes &#8212; from mom-and-pop pharmacies to large flagship clients like Carepoint.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">&#8220;One challenge facing pharmacy owners and operators is the ability to access real-time visibility into their data in a format that they chose,&#8221; said Brian Prigge, President and CTO of Scriptly. &#8220;The lack of a solution led pharmacies to make significant investments in developing one-off reports.&#8221;</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">To help pharmacies identify trends without manually creating reports on each facet of their business, Brian wanted to incorporate self-service analytics which would allow non-technical users to easily create custom dashboards and interactive data visualizations to make data-driven decisions.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" class="wp-block-heading" id="why-reveal"><strong>Why Reveal</strong> </h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Brian chose <a href="https://www.revealbi.io/embedded-analytics" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Reveal’s embedded analytics solution</a> to empower Scriptly customers with the tools they need to take their pharmacy operations to the next level. With Reveal built into Scriptly, pharmacies can access up-to-the-minute data, spot trends in patients, prescribers, and prescriptions, and customize dashboards to see relevant data. Reveal’s embedded analytics solution removed the need for manual reporting.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" class="wp-block-heading" id="identifying-medication-non-adherence"><strong>Identifying Medication Non-Adherence</strong> </h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Pharmacies can also identify medication non-adherence problems by showing prescription refill timing. According to the <a href="https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/consumer-healthcare/medication-information/medication-adherence-taking-your-meds-as-directed" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">American Heart Association</a>, it is estimated that over half the medications prescribed for people with chronic diseases are not taken as directed.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Poor medication adherence takes the lives of 125,000 Americans annually and costs the healthcare system as much as $300 billion a year in additional medical appointments, emergency department visits, and hospitalizations.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">“With Reveal’s data insights, pharmacies can see which patients are not taking their medications based on when they order refills,” Brian explained, “When a prescription says take one pill twice a day, and every other day the patient forgets to take the second pill, refill data shows pharmacies that a patient isn’t taking his or her medications as directed.”</p>
