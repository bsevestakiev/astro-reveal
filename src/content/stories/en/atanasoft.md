<h2 class="wp-block-heading" class="wp-block-heading" id="meet-atanasoft"><strong>Meet Atanasoft</strong></h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">When large government agencies, banks, manufacturers and retail companies need to access big data stored in&nbsp;Teradata&nbsp;or other data warehouses, many turn to Atanasoft. The company’s comprehensive product suites include tools and wizards that make it simpler and more efficient for consultants, IT management and general users to access and use&nbsp;data&nbsp;from large data warehouses.&nbsp;For example, a&nbsp;manufacturer can see at a glance the efficiencies of multiple production lines, the plant’s overall productivity, and the cost of labor vs revenue.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Atanasoft’s two main product lines&nbsp;are&nbsp;DataGEM for Open Database Connectivity (ODBC)-based databases&nbsp;and&nbsp;AtanaSuite&nbsp;for&nbsp;ODBC and Teradata&nbsp;warehouses. AtanaSuite is the only commercially available&nbsp;productivity suite&nbsp;that accesses Teradata warehouses in native mode.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">“Imagine almost any retail store you visit,” says Jim Richardson, president of Atanasoft.&nbsp;“When your products are scanned at the cash register, all that scanned data goes to a data warehouse somewhere. And in that&nbsp;data&nbsp;warehouse, it’s 100% certain that they’re using&nbsp;an&nbsp;application like AtanaSuite or DataGEM to manage and analyze all that data&nbsp;to make better business decisions.”</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" class="wp-block-heading" id="challenge"><strong>Challenge</strong>&nbsp;</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Atanasoft has always been at the leading edge for data analysis and&nbsp;visualizations and&nbsp;has relied upon Infragistics&nbsp;for its libraries&nbsp;of&nbsp;reliable,&nbsp;high-performance components.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Infragistics&nbsp;ensures&nbsp;its components and controls include the most advanced technology&nbsp;by&nbsp;continually&nbsp;assessing&nbsp;market changes and&nbsp;updating&nbsp;their components.&nbsp;Reveal is Infragistics most advanced&nbsp;embedded&nbsp;analytics and visualization product&nbsp;and is leading the new wave of embedded analytics software that lets users see data points in beautiful pictures for faster comprehension.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">“We wanted to add a true visualization capability as an addition to the charting capability. We were looking to make a transformation from the comprehensive data retrieval and analysis capabilities within AtanaSuite&nbsp;and&nbsp;DataGEM to dynamic information presented as user-defined dashboards.”</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" class="wp-block-heading" id="solution"><strong>Solution</strong></h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">To provide these visualizations and dashboards within its software, Atanasoft chose to embed Reveal into both AtanaSuite and DataGEM. <a href="https://www.revealbi.io/embedded-analytics" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Embedded analytics software</a> delivers real-time reporting, interactive data visualization and/or advanced analytics, including machine learning, directly into an enterprise business application.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Atanasoft selected Reveal for&nbsp;several&nbsp;reasons, including the long-term relationship with Infragistics.&nbsp;&nbsp;“We’ve developed&nbsp;a high level of trust over 20 years&nbsp;with Infragistics&nbsp;and&nbsp;we know they&nbsp;have a customizable product that meets our needs,”&nbsp;adds Richardson.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Atanasoft&nbsp;also likes&nbsp;the&nbsp;visual beauty of&nbsp;Reveal&nbsp;and how easy it&nbsp;is&nbsp;for customers to use.&nbsp;They also trust the stability and flexibility of&nbsp;Reveal’s architecture and&nbsp;the quality of its&nbsp;components. “We&nbsp;recognized the Reveal .NET architecture as being state of the art and compatible with AtanaSuite and DataGEM,” adds Richardson.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">“Our product suites are&nbsp;modular, yet integrated,” says&nbsp;Richardson.&nbsp;“All&nbsp;of our&nbsp;tools,&nbsp;helpers and wizards&nbsp;provide unique, powerful functions&nbsp;—&nbsp;yet they all work together. It’s like your&nbsp;favorite toolchest in&nbsp;the&nbsp;garage, such as&nbsp;Snap-On or Craftsman.”</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The module in the Atanasoft products that&nbsp;is based on the Reveal SDK&nbsp;is called the Dashboard Tool.&nbsp;It’s fully integrated with the same look and feel as&nbsp;the product’s other&nbsp;modules.&nbsp;When customers use the&nbsp;query tool and&nbsp;attach it&nbsp;to&nbsp;a&nbsp;Teradata or Oracle&nbsp;database&nbsp;and pull data from&nbsp;a&nbsp;DB2&nbsp;or&nbsp;SQL server, AtanaSuite&nbsp;and DataGEM&nbsp;capture the data&nbsp;and display it using the Reveal&nbsp;controls.</p>

<figure class="wp-block-pullquote is-style-default"><blockquote><p>This is the magic. On the fly, we transform data from disparate data sources into visual displays that the brain can understand and act on.</p><cite>Jim Richardson, president of Atanasoft</cite></blockquote></figure>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">What’s unique about the combination of the Atanasoft&nbsp;technology and Reveal controls is that Atanasoft’s&nbsp;Qset&nbsp;technology converts and normalizes data from multiple databases and then can display the disparate data on each separate Reveal control on a dashboard. This allows Atanasoft customers, from manufacturers to banks to local and state governments, to see in one visual display separate controls and each showing relevant, urgent data from a different source.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">“This is&nbsp;the&nbsp;magic,” says&nbsp;Richardson.&nbsp;“On the fly, we transform data from disparate data sources into visual displays&nbsp;that the brain can understand and act on.&nbsp;It’s&nbsp;why visualizations are&nbsp;so powerful.&nbsp;They integrate all the data&nbsp;in one beautiful dashboard&nbsp;—&nbsp;and our customers love it.”</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" class="wp-block-heading" id="easy-to-make-data-look-beautiful"><strong>Easy to Make Data Look Beautiful</strong></h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Reveal lets&nbsp;Atanasoft&nbsp;customers self-serve with powerful, but simple-to-use features including:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list list-disc">
<li><strong>Create visualizations quickly</strong>&nbsp;&#8212; Simply select a chart or graph and drag data columns into visualization placeholders to have it appear instantly.</li>

<li><strong>Gauges</strong> — Use linear, circular, text or KPI gauges to set dashboards apart.</li>

<li><strong>Statistical functions</strong> — Make predictions, recognize and evaluate trends, or discover data outliers using time series forecast, linear regression and&nbsp;more.</li>

<li><strong>Popular&nbsp;grids,&nbsp;graphs and&nbsp;charts</strong>&nbsp;—&nbsp;Line, area, bar, column, pivot grid, circular,&nbsp;choropleth, scatter, bubble, tree, funnel, and pie charts available.</li>

<li><strong>KPI Gauges</strong> — Use KPI Time and KPI Target gauges to make your key performance indicators stand out.</li>
</ul>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">“Customers love the dashboards because it’s their canvas —&nbsp;they create moving pictures or visualizations that their audience will understand and appreciate,” adds Richardson.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" class="wp-block-heading" id="seeing-data-in-context"><strong>Seeing Data in Context</strong></h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Atanasoft’s customers also like that they can see data in context — at the exact moment&nbsp;when they are querying databases and looking for insights.&nbsp;No need to leave&nbsp;AtanaSuite or DataGEM,&nbsp;run a separate analytics program, then come back.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">“This is what makes data analytics impactful,” says Richardson.&nbsp;“The dashboards are&nbsp;within&nbsp;our&nbsp;products,&nbsp;so&nbsp;they get seen; they are there&nbsp;<strong>at the moment</strong>&nbsp;the customer needs the information.&nbsp;So&nbsp;they can get insights, and make decisions. Ultimately that’s the power we’ve now built into our database software&nbsp;tools.”</p>
