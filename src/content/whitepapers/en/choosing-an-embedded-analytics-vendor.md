Integrating embedded analytics into your applications is a strategic decision that boosts data-driven decision-making, enhances user experience and [customer satisfaction](/blog/embedded-analytics-customer-satisfaction), and accelerates insight.

In today’s competitive and saturated markets, selecting the right embedded analytics vendor is crucial.

This guide outlines the 7 essential questions to consider, ensuring you choose a vendor that aligns with your business needs, budget, and strategic goals.

1. How intuitive and user-friendly is your embedded analytics platform for end-users?

1. What analytics capabilities and visualizations do you offer to uncover actionable insights?

1. How flexible is your embedded analytics solution in terms of customization?

1. Can the embedded analytics solution adapt to diverse application environments and industry-specific use cases?

1. What level of control do you provide over data security and compliance?

1. Do you offer comprehensive support and resources for developers during implementation and beyond?

1. Can your embedded analytics solution scale effectively with business growth and evolving data needs without increasing price?

<!-- gate -->

<h2 class="wp-block-heading whitepapers__headline-dividers" id="chapter-1">How Easy Is It for End-Users to Use Your Analytics Platform?</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Most likely, your team comprises professionals who aren’t data experts, mirroring the broader end-user base of analytics tools. Recognizing this, an intuitive and user-friendly <a href="https://www.revealbi.io/embedded-analytics" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">embedded analytics solution</a> is essential. Such a solution broadens user adoption and satisfaction, eliminating the steep learning curve often associated with complex analytics tools. By simplifying the user experience, it minimizes the demand for extensive training and support, thereby democratizing data analytics for users at every skill level.&nbsp;</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Providing self-service capabilities is crucial as it empowers users to independently explore data, <a href="https://www.revealbi.io/blog/create-high-impact-dashboards-with-embedded-analytics" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">build dashboards</a>, and generate reports. This autonomy not only boosts user engagement and productivity but also significantly alleviates the workload on your technical teams. Consequently, they can redirect their focus towards more strategic initiatives, enhancing overall organizational efficiency.&#8221;&nbsp;</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Ask the vendor:&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Is the solution’s interface easily navigable?&nbsp;&nbsp;</li>

<li>Can users build and customize dashboards and reports without IT help?&nbsp;</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading whitepapers__headline-dividers" id="chapter-2">What Analytics Features and Visualizations Do You Offer?</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The <a href="https://www.revealbi.io/whitepapers/embedded-bi-features-checklist" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">data analysis features</a> and visualization tools are pivotal in any embedded analytics solution. A wide array of features empowers users to delve into complex datasets, uncovering trends, patterns, and anomalies that drive informed decision-making. Advanced functionalities such as predictive analytics capabilities and a rich library of chart types, on the other hand, can provide deeper insights and facilitate easier data interpretation, enhancing the overall value of the analytics solution.&nbsp;</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">To evaluate the versatility of the analytics capabilities and data visualizations offered by the embedded analytics vendor, ask these questions:&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Can users filter, drill down, and link to dashboards?&nbsp;&nbsp;</li>

<li>Are there advanced analytics features, such as predictive analytics?&nbsp;</li>

<li>How extensive is the library of visualization options?&nbsp;</li>

<li>Is there support for geospatial analysis and mapping functionalities?&nbsp;</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading whitepapers__headline-dividers" id="chapter-3">How Flexible Is Your Solution for Customization?</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">When the analytics solution appears disjointed or out of place, it can significantly disrupt the user experience. This mismatch suggests a disconnect, making the analytics seem like an afterthought rather than a cohesive part of the application.&nbsp;</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">On the other hand, a highly customizable platform empowers organizations to seamlessly integrate analytics functionalities that blend with their existing applications, aligning both aesthetically and functionality to meet the unique needs of their business and users.&nbsp;</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">So, when evaluating different embedded analytics vendors, you need to keep in mind that the degree of flexibility in customization is critical. In most cases, basic customization doesn&#8217;t cut it; you need to look for extensive customization capabilities.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Ask the vendor these questions:&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Does your platform implement an API-driven approach on both the client and server sides to enable customization of available features?&nbsp;</li>

<li>Does your platform offer built-in themes that support both light and dark models?&nbsp;</li>

<li>Are users empowered with CSS or property-based theming and branding options for deeper customization?&nbsp;</li>

<li>Does your platform allow users to add custom buttons on visualizations with custom actions?&nbsp;</li>

<li>Does your platform enable users to override existing default events in the API?&nbsp;&nbsp;</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading whitepapers__headline-dividers" id="chapter-4">Can Your Solution Adapt to Different Industries and Use Cases?</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The adaptability of the embedded analytics solution to diverse application environments and industry-specific use cases is key. Each industry is unique in terms of data and analytics requirements shaped by regulatory requirements, operational processes, and market dynamics. A solution that can seamlessly integrate and accommodate these specific needs ensures its ability to deliver actionable insights that align with industry standards and best practices.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A crucial thing you should look for is the successful implementation of the embedded analytics solution in industries similar to yours or other businesses in your industry. Case studies offer evidence of the solution&#8217;s efficacy in addressing industry-specific challenges and requirements.&nbsp;</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Here are some additional questions to ask the vendor:&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Are there domain-specific analytics templates tailored to the needs of our industry?&nbsp;</li>

<li>How does the solution keep up with regulatory changes and evolving industry trends to ensure ongoing relevance and compliance?&nbsp;&nbsp;</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading whitepapers__headline-dividers" id="chapter-5">How Do You Ensure Data Security and Compliance?</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Safeguarding sensitive data and maintaining compliance with industry regulations are paramount considerations when evaluating an embedded analytics vendor. The vendor&#8217;s approach to security and compliance should be comprehensive, encompassing robust measures and adherence to relevant regulatory standards.&nbsp;</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">When speaking with the vendor, address your security concerns and ask these questions:&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Do you comply with key industry regulations such as <a href="https://gdpr-info.eu/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">GDPR</a>?&nbsp;</li>

<li>How do you manage data governance practices to ensure data integrity, availability, and confidentiality?&nbsp;</li>

<li>Does your embedded BI solution offer APIs for role-based access control?&nbsp;</li>

<li>Can you provide examples of how role-based access control is implemented within the solution to restrict user access based on their roles or responsibilities?&nbsp;</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading whitepapers__headline-dividers" id="chapter-6">What Support Do You Provide for Developers?</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Choosing the right embedded analytics vendor is about more than the tool’s features. It is also about the support it offers. Ensuring that the vendor offers robust assistance is crucial, particularly during the Proof of Concept (PoC) phases. Make sure that the vendor will provide you with the resources you need to overcome any technical challenges. Comprehensive documentation, tutorials, and support resources for developers are crucial for ensuring a smooth integration process and ongoing maintenance.&nbsp;</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Here are a few questions to ask the vendor to evaluate the support it provides to developers:&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>How detailed and accessible is the documentation, and does it include guides, tutorials, API references, and troubleshooting resources?&nbsp;&nbsp;</li>

<li>What kind of technical support do you provide during the integration process and after deployment?&nbsp;&nbsp;</li>

<li>Is there direct access to technical experts to help resolve integration challenges and address technical issues in case such occur? And is such access available during the PoC phase?&nbsp;&nbsp;</li>

<li>Do you host active developer communities or forums, and how engaged is your technical team?&nbsp; &nbsp;</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading whitepapers__headline-dividers" id="chapter-7">Can Your Solution Scale Without Increasing Costs?</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Scalability is the cornerstone of providing long-term viability and efficiency in any embedded analytics solution. As businesses grow and evolve, they generate increasing amounts of data and face increasing demands for timely and detailed insights from users. The <a href="https://www.revealbi.io/embedded-analytics" target="_blank" rel="noopener">embedded analytics solution</a> you choose to integrate within your apps must be able to accommodate this growth seamlessly without causing disruptions.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">And that scalability should also go hand in hand with cost-effectiveness. As your user base and app usage grows, you need assurance that scaling up will not lead to unexpected and rising costs.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The tricky part is that many of the vendors in the embedded analytics market use <a href="https://www.revealbi.io/blog/embedded-bi-cost-models-the-need-for-pricing-transparency-and-predictability" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">unpredictable pricing structure techniques,</a> often charging based on a per-user-, per-dashboard call, or per-application model. This approach maximizes the price of the product while leaving ‘’no money on the table’’. A transparent and predictable pricing model is essential for organizations that are looking to scale, drive business growth, and maximize the value derived from their data assets without compromising financial stability. </p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">When the conversation with the vendor comes to the pricing part, ask these questions:&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>How much does the solution cost?&nbsp;&nbsp;</li>

<li>Do you charge on a per-user, per-dashboard call, or per-application?&nbsp;&nbsp;</li>

<li>What are your cloud usage and per-user fees?&nbsp;&nbsp;</li>

<li>Are there other fees that our organization should be aware of?&nbsp;</li>

<li>Can the solution be deployed for local or cloud-based hosting on Windows or Linux containers?&nbsp;</li>
</ul>
