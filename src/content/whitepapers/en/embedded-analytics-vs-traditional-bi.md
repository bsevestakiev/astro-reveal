Now more than ever, businesses are inundated with data, requiring robust tools for analysis, decision-making and driving growth. The landscape of data analytics is dominated by two models: Embedded Analytics and Traditional Business Intelligence (BI).

Each offers distinct benefits and could be pivotal in leveraging data to drive business success.

This whitepaper delves into these approaches, providing a comprehensive comparison to aid in selecting the optimal tool for your analytics needs.

<h2 class="wp-block-heading whitepapers__headline-dividers" id="chapter-1">What Is Traditional Business Intelligence (BI)?</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://www.revealbi.io/glossary/business-intelligence" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Traditional Business Intelligence</a> (BI) encompasses a suite of tools and processes developed over decades to analyze and present business data. It supports strategic decision-making through structured data extraction, analysis, and reporting. Traditionally, BI systems are standalone solutions requiring distinct operational efforts from the core business processes, often requiring specialized technical expertise for effective management and utilization.&nbsp;</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Key characteristics of traditional BI:&nbsp;</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Data silos:</strong> BI tools often operate independently from daily business operations, leading to data silos.&nbsp;</li>

<li><strong>Technical expertise:</strong> Primarily managed by IT professionals, necessitating a gap between data analysts and other business users.&nbsp;</li>

<li><strong>Limited sharing and collaboration:</strong> Challenges in sharing insights across the organization due to system isolation.&nbsp;</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading whitepapers__headline-dividers" id="chapter-2">What Is Embedded Analytics?</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Embedded Analytics integrates analytics capabilities directly into business applications, enabling real-time data analysis and decision-making within the workflow. This approach aligns analytics with everyday business operations, enhancing accessibility and immediacy of insights.&nbsp;</p>

<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img fetchpriority="high" decoding="async" width="1024" height="688" src="/images/2020/11/benefits-of-embedded-analytics.png" alt="embedded analytics advantages for businesses" title="Embedded Analytics vs. Traditional BI: Choosing the Right Path for Your Data Needs " /></figure>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading whitepapers__headline-dividers" id="chapter-3">What Are the Benefits of Embedded Analytics?</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://www.revealbi.io/embedded-analytics" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Embedded analytics offers several advantages</a> over Traditional BI, making it an attractive choice for organizations seeking dynamic and data-rich solutions: </p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Enhanced user experience</strong>: Direct integration into applications improves efficiency and reduces the need for multiple tools.&nbsp;</li>

<li><strong>User empowerment</strong>: Facilitates self-service analytics, allowing non-technical users to <a href="https://www.revealbi.io/blog/reveal-data-driven-decision-making" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">make data-driven decisions</a> without IT intervention, reducing bottlenecks.&nbsp;</li>

<li><strong>Consistent branding</strong>: Offers <a href="https://www.revealbi.io/blog/white-label-analytics" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">customizable interfaces</a> that align with the application&#8217;s look and feel.&nbsp;</li>

<li><strong>Cost efficiency</strong>: Predictable pricing models and reduced infrastructure needs lower total cost of ownership.&nbsp;</li>

<li><strong>Agility and scalability</strong>: Quick deployment and easy adaptation to evolving business requirements, gaining a competitive advantage.&nbsp;</li>
</ul>
