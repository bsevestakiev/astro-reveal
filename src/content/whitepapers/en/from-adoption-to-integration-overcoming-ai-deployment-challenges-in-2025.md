The 2025 Reveal Survey confirms that while businesses have widely adopted AI, many are struggling with execution, security, and workforce challenges. The competitive landscape is shifting, and the companies that fail to master AI deployment will fall behind.

Beyond AI, business intelligence (BI) and embedded analytics are becoming essential for real-time decision-making. Companies that fail to leverage data-driven insights risk falling behind competitors who are optimizing operations, increasing efficiency, and driving revenue growth.

This whitepaper presents key insights from the 2025 Reveal Survey, highlighting the biggest challenges and growth opportunities facing CTOs and business leaders today.

<h2 class="wp-block-heading whitepapers__headline-dividers" id="chapter-4">Key Findings &#038; Analysis </h2>

<h3 class="h2 wp-block-heading">1. AI Deployment: From Adoption to Execution Struggles</h3>

<p class="h5 text-primary wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Overview</strong></p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The biggest shift in 2025 is the move from <a href='/reveal-survey-report-top-software-development-challenges-for-2025'>AI adoption to execution challenges</a>. In 2024, businesses focused on integrating AI, but by 2025, execution failures, security risks, and workforce shortages have overtaken adoption as the primary concerns.</p>

<p class="h5 text-primary wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Key Statistics</strong></p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>55%</strong> of tech leaders say AI deployment is their biggest challenge.</li>
  <li><strong>45%</strong> report AI code reliability issues.</li>
  <li><strong>44%</strong> still struggle with AI rollout complexity.</li>
</ul>

<p class="h5 text-primary wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Reason</strong></p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Many companies adopted AI expecting automation and efficiency gains, but without a clear data strategy, governance framework, and scalable infrastructure, execution failures emerged. AI models require constant monitoring, refinement, and security protocols, leading to deployment bottlenecks.</p>

<p class="h5 text-primary wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Analysis</strong></p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Having AI is no longer a competitive edge—making it work effectively and securely is now the challenge. Companies that failed to plan for maintenance, security, and AI reliability are now dealing with high costs, performance issues, and operational setbacks.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">&nbsp;</p>

<figure class="wp-block-pullquote is-style-default"><blockquote><p>“AI deployment at scale isn’t just a technical hurdle—it’s an enterprise-wide challenge. Too many organizations implement AI in isolated pockets rather than integrating it across their business. Without a clear strategy for governance, execution, and sustainability, they risk inefficiency, compliance issues, and missed ROI.”</p><cite>Kurt Petersen, SVP of Customer Success at Camunda, &#8220;AI Deployment Challenges,&#8221; MSN, 2025</cite></blockquote></figure>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">&nbsp;</p>

<p class="h5 text-primary wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Summary</strong></p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">AI has shifted from an innovation driver to an operational challenge. Companies must now focus on stabilizing AI workflows, improving reliability, and securing AI applications. Those that fail to refine implementation will fall behind competitors who prioritize optimization.</p>

<h3 class="h2 mt-5 wp-block-heading">2. Security Takes Center Stage</h3>

<p class="h5 text-primary wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Overview</strong></p>
<p>Security is now the top software concern in 2025. As AI becomes embedded in business processes, automated cyber threats, AI-generated vulnerabilities, and regulatory risks have escalated. Attackers are evolving faster than security teams can adapt, making proactive AI security strategies a necessity.</p>

<p class="h5 text-primary wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Key Statistics</strong></p>
<ul>
    <li><strong>51%</strong> of organizations rank security as their top concern.</li>
    <li><strong>41%</strong> cite data privacy as a growing challenge.</li>
</ul>

<p class="h5 text-primary wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Reason</strong></p>
<p>AI-generated code often lacks built-in security validation, exposing companies to data breaches, unauthorized access, and automated cyberattacks. Additionally, AI-driven cyber threats—deepfake scams, phishing automation, and synthetic fraud—are increasing in sophistication, making traditional security defenses ineffective.</p>

<p class="h5 text-primary wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Analysis</strong></p>
<p>Security can no longer be an afterthought—it must be integrated into AI development from the start. Companies need real-time threat detection, AI auditing, and compliance-driven security measures to stay ahead of evolving threats.</p>

<p class="h5 text-primary wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Summary</strong></p>
<p>AI-driven security risks have transformed cybersecurity from an IT concern to a business imperative. Organizations that fail to adopt security-first AI practices risk major breaches, regulatory fines, and operational failures.</p>

<h3 class="h2 mt-5 wp-block-heading">3. The Tech Talent Crisis Deepens</h3>

<p class="h5 text-primary wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Overview</strong></p>
<p>The AI talent shortage, which saw some improvement in 2024, has worsened in 2025—especially in AI and cybersecurity roles. Companies that rapidly adopted AI now lack the specialized workforce needed to scale, refine, and secure their AI-driven infrastructure.</p>

<p class="h5 text-primary wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Key Statistics</strong></p>
<ul>
    <li><strong>48%</strong> of tech leaders say hiring AI and cybersecurity talent is a major challenge.</li>
    <li><strong>63%</strong> say AI expertise is their most critical hiring need.</li>
    <li><strong>55%</strong> of companies created new AI-related roles.</li>
</ul>

<p class="h5 text-primary wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Reason</strong></p>
<p>AI&#8217;s rapid expansion has outpaced workforce readiness. Many companies assumed generalist developers could handle AI, but now realize that specialized expertise—AI engineers, data scientists, and cybersecurity specialists—is essential. The demand for AI and security professionals far exceeds supply.</p>

<p class="h5 text-primary wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Analysis</strong></p>
<p>The talent shortage is now a direct roadblock to AI execution. Without skilled professionals to manage AI governance, security, and optimization, businesses are experiencing stalled innovation, higher risks, and operational inefficiencies.</p>

<p class="h5 text-primary wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Summary</strong></p>
<p>This isn’t just a hiring problem—it’s an industry-wide crisis. Companies must invest in AI training, upskill existing employees, and prioritize specialized hiring to sustain long-term AI success.</p>

<h3 class="h2 mt-5 wp-block-heading">4. Business Growth Exceeds Expectations</h3>

<p class="h5 text-primary wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Overview</strong></p>
<p>Despite AI deployment struggles, companies that strategically implemented AI have seen significant revenue growth and increased project opportunities. Businesses that took a structured, security-first approach experienced the highest efficiency and profitability gains.</p>

<p class="h5 text-primary wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Key Statistics</strong></p>
<ul>
    <li><strong>80%</strong> of companies saw revenue growth.</li>
    <li><strong>82%</strong> took on new projects in 2024.</li>
</ul>

<p class="h5 text-primary wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Reason</strong></p>
<p>Organizations that avoided rushed AI rollouts and focused on scalable AI integration, automation, and analytics-driven decision-making saw the strongest financial benefits.</p>

<p class="h5 text-primary wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Analysis</strong></p>
<p>AI adoption alone isn’t enough—businesses that optimize execution, security, and workforce training are outperforming expectations. Companies that failed to address these areas are seeing inefficiencies and missed opportunities.</p>

<p class="h5 text-primary wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Summary</strong></p>
<p>AI-driven growth is possible, but only for companies that focus on execution, security, and workforce readiness. Businesses that refine AI strategies and invest in scalable, secure AI applications will continue to see financial gains.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading whitepapers__headline-dividers" id="chapter-5">How Reveal AI is Bridging the Industry Gap with Conversational Analytics</h2>

<h2 class="h2 wp-block-heading" class="h2 wp-block-heading" id="the-accessibility-challenge-in-traditional-analytics">The Accessibility Challenge in Traditional Analytics</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">One of the biggest gaps in traditional analytics is accessibility—most BI tools require specialized knowledge, SQL queries, or manual report-building, making it difficult for non-technical users to extract meaningful insights.</p>
<p>Even with embedded analytics, users still need to navigate dashboards, apply filters, and analyze data before drawing conclusions. Historically, this process can be slow, technical, and prone to human error.</p>

<h2 class="h2 wp-block-heading" class="h2 wp-block-heading" id="conversational-analytics-a-new-approach">Conversational Analytics: A New Approach</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Conversational analytics changes this paradigm. Instead of manually sorting through data, applying filters, and generating reports, users can simply ask direct questions in natural language and receive AI-powered insights instantly.</p>
<p>Reveal AI is at the forefront of this transformation, enabling businesses to:</p>
<ul>
    <li>Access insights instantly: Conversational queries replace static dashboards, allowing users to get answers without navigating complex interfaces.</li>
    <li>Eliminate manual analysis: AI automatically uncovers trends, anomalies, and key insights, removing the need for users to manually analyze data.</li>
    <li>Real-Time AI-Driven Decision Support: Automated analytics proactively identifies patterns, trends, and opportunities, giving businesses a competitive edge.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="h2 wp-block-heading" class="h2 wp-block-heading" id="how-it-works">How it Works</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Conversational analytics functions like an AI-powered assistant—think of it as a ChatGPT-style prompt menu, but designed to generate precise, real-time insights directly from your data based on your specific queries. Instead of navigating complex dashboards, users can type a specific question into a prompt and receive immediate, precise answers without needing to manually filter reports or analyze raw data.</p>
<p>For example:</p>
<ul>
    <li>Instead of searching through multiple reports to find monthly revenue trends, a user can simply ask: &#8220;What were our top-performing products last quarter?&#8221;</li>
    <li>Rather than applying filters manually, a user can ask: &#8220;Show me sales growth by region over the last six months.&#8221;</li>
</ul>
<p>With Reveal AI, users no longer need technical expertise to find critical insights—they ask, and the system provides answers. This removes the complexity of traditional analytics and ensures that every decision-maker has access to real-time, AI-driven insights without relying on data teams or BI specialists.</p>
